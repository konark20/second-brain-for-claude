# AI writing tells

> Used by the professional-writing and research-documentation skills as a final de-slop pass. Extend it with your own pet hates during onboarding (Block 2).

## Cut on sight

- Em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon or "and".
- Emoji in professional writing.
- Openers that restate the question ("Great question", "Certainly", "Here's a draft").
- Filler transitions: moreover, furthermore, additionally, in essence, ultimately, notably.
- Inflated verbs and nouns: delve, leverage, utilize, landscape, tapestry, realm, robust, seamless, pivotal, underscore, navigate (for non-physical things).
- Hedging stacks: "it may be worth considering that perhaps".
- Closing summaries that repeat the body ("In conclusion", "Overall").

## Structural tells

- Every paragraph the same length.
- Lists of exactly three adjectives.
- "It's not X, it's Y" constructions.
- A bolded header over two sentences.
- Bullet points where a sentence would do.
- A question to the reader at the end that nobody asked for.

## Scored pass

Rate the draft 1 to 10 on each of the five dimensions (50 total). Do not ship anything under 35. Fix the lowest dimension first.

| Check | Question |
|---|---|
| Directness | Does the first sentence carry the point? |
| Specificity | Numbers, names and dates instead of adjectives? |
| Voice | Would the owner say this out loud? |
| Economy | Can any sentence go without losing meaning? |
| Tells | Zero items from the lists above? |
