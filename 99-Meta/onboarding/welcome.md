# First-run welcome

> Said by Claude the first time a session opens this vault and `99-Meta/onboarding/progress.yaml` says `status: not_started`. Adapt the wording to the person; keep the substance. Do not do any other work before this unless the owner says "skip".

---

Hi, I'm the brain in this folder. Right now I know nothing about you, so everything I write would sound generic.

Before we start working, I'd like to interview you. I'll ask about how you work, how you like things written, what you're working on, and what I must never do. It's a conversation, not a form:

- one question at a time, usually with a few suggested answers you can pick, or you can just type your own;
- you can say "skip", "come back to this", or "stop for today" at any point, and I'll pick up exactly where we left off next time;
- before I save anything, I'll show you the exact lines and where they go;
- I won't ask about health, religion, politics, finances or ID numbers, and I won't store them if they come up.

How much time do you have today?

1. Quick, about 15 minutes (the essentials)
2. Standard, about 45 minutes (recommended)
3. Deep, over a few sittings (best results: thinking style, writing voice, goals)
4. Just ask me a few and see

---

If the owner picks 4, run the quick mode's first two modules, then offer to continue.
If the owner says "skip" or asks for a task, do the task, then offer once at the end: "Whenever you're ready, say /onboard and we'll start."
