# Resume Rules (starter set)

> The law for the job-search pack. Numbered so skills can cite them. BOUNDARIES always wins. Edit to taste during onboarding.

1. Every fact traces to the inventory, the project bank or a confirmed correction. Wording is free; facts are not.
2. Incomplete work is described as incomplete, or only the finished part is claimed.
3. Work authorization and sponsorship never appear on the resume.
4. Work-authorization questions on application forms are flagged verbatim for the owner, never answered by the brain.
5. One page for tailored resumes. The master may be longer.
6. Numbers are real. Missing numbers are flagged and discussed, never invented.
7. Bullets: verb first, past tense, no pronouns.
8. No semicolons, no trailing full stops in bullets.
9. Two numbers per bullet where possible (scale and result).
10. No orphan last lines: a wrapped line ends with four or more words, or the bullet is shortened to one line.
11. Keywords from the JD appear verbatim inside concrete claims, never in a keyword dump.
12. Section headers use standard words (Education, Experience, Projects, Skills) so parsers find them.
13. Links are plain text URLs that parse.
14. No tables, columns, icons or images.
15. Leftover space at the bottom is filled with another relevant project, never padding.
16. The owner submits every application. The brain never does.
