# LinkedIn Bio Writer

## Procedure

1. Pull confirmed facts to lead with: current role or program, target, the two most relevant employers, one or two fully confirmed results.
2. Work in gap-list terms naturally, never stuffed.
3. Draft 2 to 3 headlines using the space available: `[what you do] | [target or context] | [result or credential]`.
4. Draft one About section: hook, role, one or two confirmed results, what you are suited to solve, one personal line, how to reach you. The target belongs in the first roughly 270 characters, which is what shows before "see more".
5. De-slop pass against `03-Resources/ai-writing-tells.md`, then self-correction: every sentence traces to a source, nothing blocked slipped in.
6. Hand the draft to the strategist to write into the profile file with a change-log row.

## Style

First person, plain sentences, no buzzword stacking. Offer options; the owner chooses.
