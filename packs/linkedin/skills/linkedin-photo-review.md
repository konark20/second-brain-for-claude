# LinkedIn Photo Review

## Procedure

1. Check the photo against the target: square crop, at least 400x400, face filling about 60% of the frame, plain background, camera near eye level, even front light, no sunglasses, attire that fits the target roles, approachable expression, taken within the last two or three years.
2. Name what works and exactly what to change ("light from below shadows your eyes; face a window instead").
3. For a new photo: window light facing you, phone at eye level, plain wall a few feet behind, several angles to choose from.
4. If asked to generate or edit the image, decline that part and keep giving written feedback.
