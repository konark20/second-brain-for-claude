# LinkedIn rules (starter set)

1. Every claim traces to the inventory or a confirmed correction.
2. Drafts only. The owner clicks save and post.
3. No work-authorization text. No unconfirmed numbers.
4. No generated or edited photos of the owner.
5. If two sources disagree, log both and ask.
6. Change log row for every edit: date, who, what, applied.
7. Tone: first person, plain, specific. No em dashes, emoji or hashtag stacks.
